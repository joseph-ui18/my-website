RAID Concepts

What is RAID?

RAID (Redundant Array of Independent Disks) is a technology that combines multiple physical disks into a logical unit to improve performance, provide redundancy, or both.

Common levels include RAID 0 (striping), RAID 1 (mirroring), and RAID 5/10 (striping with parity or mirroring plus striping).
