About Multimedia projects

A multimedia project combines video, audio, graphics, animation, and interaction into a single cohesive product.

This page focuses on planning, organizing assets, and sequencing your media for the best learner experience.

A good multimedia project includes a clear introduction, structured content, and a strong conclusion or call to action.

Project Notes & Observations

Create a storyboard or script before building to keep your content organized and aligned with your goals.

Review and revise your project so visuals and audio support the message without overwhelming the viewer.
