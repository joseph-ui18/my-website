Principles of Animation

About Animation basics

Animation brings still elements to life by using motion, timing, and transitions to guide the audience through a sequence.

Explore the core principles of animation such as easing, anticipation, timing, and smoothing motion for natural movement.

Even simple animation can make diagrams, text, and interfaces more engaging when it is used intentionally.

Animation Lab Notes

Plan your animation frame by frame or keyframe by keyframe to keep movement clear and purposeful.

Pay attention to timing and spacing to make motion feel smooth rather than sudden or jarring.
