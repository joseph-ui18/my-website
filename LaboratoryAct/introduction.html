What Is Multimedia Authoring?

About Multimedia authoring

Multimedia authoring combines text, audio, video, graphics, and interactivity to create rich learning experiences and digital projects.

This page introduces core concepts and explains how different media types work together to tell a story or teach a skill.

Use this introduction to establish goals for your project, define your audience, and choose the tools you need for each media type.

Getting Started

Start by outlining your multimedia project, including the message, structure, and media assets you will need.

Good planning improves consistency, accessibility, and the overall impact of your multimedia presentation.
