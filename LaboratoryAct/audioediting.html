Editing Audio for Multimedia

About Audio editing

Audio editing involves cleaning, trimming, and mixing sounds so voice, music, and effects work together smoothly.

Good audio editing improves clarity, removes distractions, and gives your project a polished sonic character.

Focus on noise reduction, leveling, and proper fade-ins/fade-outs to make your audio feel professional.

Audio Workflow Notes

When editing audio, monitor levels closely and use EQ to remove harsh frequencies.

Test your final mix on multiple playback systems to confirm it sounds balanced in different environments.
