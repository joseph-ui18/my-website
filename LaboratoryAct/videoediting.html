Editing Video for Storytelling

About Video editing

Video editing brings raw footage together with pacing, transitions, and audio to create a polished final piece.

Learn basic editing workflows such as trimming clips, arranging scenes, and adding simple effects that support your message.

Effective video editing balances visuals and sound to keep the viewer engaged from start to finish.

Key Editing Principles

Organize your clips before editing, and choose the best takes to build a clear narrative flow.

Use cuts, transitions, and timing to maintain energy and focus on the most important content.
