Working in 3D Space

About 3d animation

3D animation creates motion using models, rigs, and cameras in a three-dimensional environment.

Learn how to set up simple 3D scenes, animate objects, and use keyframes to control movement and timing.

3D animation adds depth and realism, but it also requires careful planning of camera movement and scene composition.

3D Animation Notes

Focus on clean key poses and smooth interpolation to make motion appear natural.

Consider lighting and camera angles as part of your animation design.
