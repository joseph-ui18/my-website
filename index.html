Multimedia & Authoring

What is Multimedia?

Multimedia is the combination of different media elements such as text, images, audio, video, and animation to deliver information in an engaging way.

It is widely used in websites, games, films, and educational platforms to improve user experience and communication.

Video Editing

Video editing involves arranging video clips, adding effects, transitions, and audio to produce a complete video project.

Audio Editing

Audio editing focuses on recording, trimming, and enhancing sound quality using digital tools.

Graphic Design

Graphic design is the creation of visual content using typography, images, and layout techniques.

Animation

Animation is the process of creating motion graphics and visual effects using sequential images.

Authoring Tools

Authoring tools are software used to create interactive multimedia applications such as e-learning modules and presentations.

Final Activity

You will create a complete multimedia project combining video, audio, graphics, and animation into one interactive output.
